Philip Huffman writes about Stoicism, civic life, and the practical work
of getting unstuck. His credo: Think clearly. Live deliberately. Love
intensely. guides everything he creates, offering a framework for
living with clarity, proportion, and authentic connection.

A U.S. Army veteran who served between 1973 and 1975, Huffman brings a
unique perspective to his exploration of human agency, resilience, and
the quiet work of building character. His writing speaks to readers who
have outgrown conventional advice, blending personal experience with
philosophical inquiry to illuminate the path to intentional living.

Whether exploring metabolic health through Stoic principles, the
challenges of neurodivergence, or the moral dimensions of civic life,
Huffman’s work consistently returns to the same themes: clarity as the
foundation of effective action, discipline as the path to authentic
freedom, and love as the force that makes both possible.
